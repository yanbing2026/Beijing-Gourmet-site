Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit. If you still have a question, call us and we'll be happy to help.

Do you have crab legs on your buffet?

No, we do not offer crab legs. Instead, we have crawfish, jumbo shrimp, and blue crab available during DINNER ONLY. Small shrimp and mussels are available during both lunch and dinner.

How late are you open?

We are open from 11:00 AM every day. We close at 8:30 PM Sunday through Thursday, and at 9:00 PM Friday and Saturday. Our kitchen, sushi bar, and Mongolian Grill close 30 minutes before closing time.

Is there a time limit for the all-you-can-eat buffet?

Yes, 2 hours. However, we may make exceptions for parties with over six people and patrons who have stopped eating before the time limit during non-busy hours.

Do you have vegetarian, vegan, halal or allergy-friendly and gluten-free options?

Our vegetarian and vegan selection is very limited, especially during dinner, but our Mongolian Grill has a wide selection of vegetables. We have chicken, beef, and shrimp on the buffet. No pork. We also do not use peanut oil. For those who are sensitive or intolerant to gluten, please be aware that a lot of our buffet selection has or contains traces of gluten. If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, please call ahead or let us know at the front desk before you dine.

We also have an à la carte menu available separately from the dine-in all-you-can-eat buffet.

Still have a question?

Call us before your visit and we'll help you plan.
