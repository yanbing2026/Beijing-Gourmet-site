FAQ

Do you have crab legs on your buffet?

No, we do not offer crab legs. Instead, we have crawfish, jumbo shrimp, and blue crab available during DINNER ONLY. Small shrimp and mussels are available during both lunch and dinner.

How late are you open?

We close at 5:00 PM every day, except for Friday and Saturday — our hours are extended till 9:00 PM on those days. Our kitchen, sushi bar, and Mongolian Grill are closed 30 minutes before closing time. For a better experience, please come early for the whole buffet selection (preferably before 7:00 PM).

Is there a time limit for the all-you-can-eat buffet?

Yes, 2 hours. However, we may make exceptions for parties with over six people and patrons who have stopped eating before the time limit during non-busy hours.

Do you have vegetarian, vegan, halal or allergy-friendly and gluten-free options?

Our vegetarian and vegan selection is very limited (especially during dinner), but our Mongolian Grill has a wide selection of vegetables for you to choose from. We have chicken, beef, and shrimp on the buffet. No pork. We also do not use peanut oil. For those who are sensitive or intolerant to gluten, please be aware that a lot of our buffet selection has or contains traces of gluten. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please call ahead of time or let us know at the front desk before you dine.

* Note: We have an à la carte menu available separate from the dine-in all-you-can-eat buffet.
